Crew setup and trip checklist

Version 1.13.4. Update the existing MAPA app in place; keep its saved connection. A new crew phone registers with its own real details.

Download the signed MAPA 1.13.4 APK

Server in MAPA: https://app.itda.asrhealth.in (no extra path). Enable installation for the installing browser or file manager only if Android asks; keep Play Protect enabled. No USB debugging is required.

1. Connect the crew phone

  1. Register once. Open Register / connect to ASR Health. Enter your actual name, contact email, phone, ambulance registration/name and hospital/base. Use a distinct real email. For a rejected duplicate-email request, choose Edit rejected registration, correct your own driver email, and retake selfie/GPS. Use existing account returns to password sign-in. Existing accounts are preserved; pending or approved connections need control review.
  2. Send the selfie and location. Read the collection notice, consent, allow the camera and precise location, switch phone Location on, and take a new selfie. Submit and keep the app installed while approval is pending.
  3. Wait for verified approval. Check status after control approves your actual identity, ambulance and base. Confirm your displayed name and ambulance before working. Registration does not start duty.
  4. Start duty explicitly. From Home choose Start duty / Resume location sharing, then Start sharing. Allow notifications. Wait for a recent GPS receipt and ask control to verify your correct ambulance on its map.
  5. Test this handset. Phone setup's Test alert sound is a local 30-second test with a Stop button; it creates no call. Someone near the phone must confirm sound. Keep duty/location sharing and network available.

2. Handle the assigned trip

  1. Check, then accept. Control enters the existing telephone call manually and explicitly dispatches an eligible crew. Open/check your assigned alert to stop repeat sound. Read the pickup, callback and destination, then tap Accept this call. Checking alone does not accept it.
  2. Confirm actual progress. Wait for fresh GPS received after acceptance. Confirm the offered response-start and transport stages only when they happen.
  3. Record actual handover. At the receiving point, confirm handover with the team, enter a trip-end note and take a new photo of the ambulance or safe handover area. Avoid patient faces or documents. GPS coordinates/time/accuracy are captured automatically.
  4. Wait for completion receipt. Keep precise GPS and network available. Submit and wait for MAPA to confirm. An uncertain update must retry the exact saved action. Recapture only after a definite stale-evidence rejection; do not replace an uncertain action.
  5. End duty separately. After confirmed completion and no active assignment, tap End duty and wait for its receipt. GPS stops after duty-off confirmation. Do not end duty while trip completion is uncertain.

Command-center checklist

Drivers → Crew registration: review the driver's details and private selfie, verify identity, select the matching actual ambulance and hospital/base (or add the missing actual records in the review form first), confirm identity and approve the connection. Do not approve guessed records or take over an existing vehicle assignment. The operator desk's Crew registration view is also available.

Verify recent received GPS for the approved vehicle before dispatch. Review acknowledgement and trip progress, then Trip reports for the private note/photo and GPS evidence. Check duty-off. Test 3 phones, then 10, then 25; software tests alone do not prove handset or field acceptance.

Alert and connection limits: polling needs MAPA open or its explicitly started duty service. Firebase cold-app push is not configured. Silent/DND, notification channels, permissions, battery rules, force-stop or missing network can prevent ringing. Reopen MAPA and explicitly resume sharing after reboot or interruption. Use normal telephone coordination if an alert/update is missing.

Privacy and support: ASR Health stores submitted driver/contact/ambulance/base details and a private selfie; explicit duty sharing records GPS, and completion stores a private note/photo/location report. A selfie is for staff review, not automated biometric verification. Contact control for corrections, lost phones, existing-email conflicts or reconnection. New registration/photo/test forms currently use English.

The shared server currently depends on its Windows PC, Docker, power and internet. The APK does not create always-on hosting. GPS and photographs are records for review, not independent proof of patient handover. Full 25-phone acceptance and dependable hosting remain required before operational reliance.
