Using MAPA

The command centre is for authorized ambulance operations. Use your own approved account, protect its credentials and provide accurate information. Do not impersonate another person, access unrelated records or attempt to bypass access restrictions.

Calls, assignments and tracking

Saving a call, opening a tracking link or receiving a notification does not confirm that an ambulance has accepted an assignment. Operators and crews must verify the pickup, availability, destination and status through the established response process. Location may be delayed or unavailable because of GPS reception, device power or network conditions. A displayed position is not a guaranteed arrival time.

Patient information

Only authorized personnel should submit or view patient-related information necessary for a trip or handover. Verify the destination and recipient before sharing. Clinical assessment and treatment remain the responsibility of qualified professionals.

Emergency use and outages

If the website, tracking or alerts are unavailable, use ASR Health’s established telephone and operational fallback arrangements. Do not wait for an app notification when urgent assistance is required. The operator must publish the verified emergency and support contact details for its service.
