Privacy policy

Draft for operator review. ASR Health must confirm its legal entity, privacy contact, legal bases, retention periods and service providers before this notice is published as its final policy.

What this service does

MAPA is the ambulance operations software used by ASR Health. Authorized staff coordinate calls, ambulances, crews and hospital handovers. This notice concerns the command centre and its related crew and caller-location features.

Information handled

Depending on the feature used, records can include account and contact details, callback numbers, pickup and destination information, ambulance and device identifiers, GPS positions and times, trip status, patient-condition notes, and crew-submitted photographs or reports. Only submit information needed for the response.

Location sharing

A caller link requests browser location permission and an explicit sharing confirmation. You can stop sharing using the Stop sharing control or your browser permissions. Collection of your phone location ends after pickup. A journey link can then continue showing the assigned ambulance and receiving hospital until completion, revocation or expiry; older pickup-only links end at pickup. Treat links received by SMS or WhatsApp as private: anyone holding a valid journey link may view its limited trip information and available driver, callback and hospital contact numbers. The call control opens your phone dialler; it does not place a call automatically. Stopping sharing does not erase information already received. Crew phones and installed vehicle trackers have separate location-reporting workflows; a vehicle fix does not prove a crew member is on duty.

Access and service providers

Account and role checks restrict operational records. The command centre is hosted on a Hostinger VPS. Compatibility traffic may pass through Cloudflare. Map requests may contact OpenStreetMap or an enabled map provider. Messaging and push providers must be disclosed when activated; a planned integration should not be represented as already operating.

Browser storage

The application uses authentication/session storage and functional preferences to operate. Caller tracking uses a temporary trip capability. Do not forward a private trip link. Any additional analytics or advertising technology requires a separate review and appropriate controls before activation.

Retention and security

Access controls and HTTPS protect parts of the service, but no system guarantees absolute security. ASR Health must set and publish retention periods for call records, location history, uploaded evidence, logs and backups, including any legally required retention. No automatic deletion period is promised in this draft.

Requests and questions

The operator must provide a verified privacy contact before launch. Requests may concern access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights. Identity verification and lawful retention requirements can affect a request. Do not send patient records through an unverified contact channel.
