Data rights and GDPR

Operator review pending. This information page is not a certification of GDPR compliance. Applicability, legal bases and the operator’s response process must be assessed before launch.

When GDPR applies

Whether GDPR applies depends on the organization’s establishment and activities, including relevant services offered to or monitoring of people in the EU. Hosting location or adding a policy page alone does not resolve that question.

Rights where applicable

Depending on the legal conditions, individuals may request information and access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, that consent can be withdrawn. Individuals may also complain to the relevant supervisory authority. These rights have conditions and exceptions.

Location and patient information

Precise location and patient-condition information require particular care. The operator must document its processing purposes, appropriate legal bases, access restrictions and any additional conditions for health data. Browser location permission is not a substitute for that assessment.

Before publication

ASR Health must confirm the responsible legal entity and privacy contact; retention and deletion rules; processors and transfers; the process for verified rights requests; incident response; and whether a data-protection impact assessment or designated privacy role is required. The software must be tested against those decisions.

Further information

See the European Commission’s explanation of individual rights and the GDPR text. Read our draft privacy policy for the currently identified application data flows.
